Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

KosovaGo is a travel and discovery application designed to help users explore restaurants, events, attractions and destinations throughout Kosovo.

We respect your privacy and are committed to handling personal information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use KosovaGo, we may collect the following information:

  • Account Information: Email addresses and unique account identifiers used for registration, authentication and account management.
  • Favourites: Places, attractions or events you choose to save to your account.
  • Reviews: Reviews, ratings and other content you voluntarily submit through the application.
  • AI Assistant Messages: Questions and conversation history submitted when using Kosovo Guide.
  • Support Communications: Information you provide when contacting us for assistance.

You can browse available destinations and attractions without necessarily providing all the information listed above.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Authenticate users and maintain account security.
  • Save and display favourites.
  • Publish and manage user-submitted reviews.
  • Provide AI-powered travel recommendations.
  • Respond to enquiries and support requests.
  • Maintain, protect and improve the reliability of the application.

3. Kosovo Guide — AI Assistant

KosovaGo includes an AI-powered assistant called Kosovo Guide, which helps users discover places, restaurants, activities and events in Kosovo.

When you use Kosovo Guide, your messages and relevant conversation history are transmitted to our application backend and processed using services provided by OpenAI to generate responses.

Please avoid submitting sensitive personal information, such as passwords, financial information or medical details, through the AI assistant.

AI-generated recommendations may occasionally be inaccurate or outdated. Users should independently verify important information before making travel arrangements.

4. Account Management and Data Storage

KosovaGo uses Supabase to support account authentication, database functionality and the storage of certain application information.

Depending on the features you use, this may include account identifiers, email addresses, favourites and user-submitted reviews.

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect information against unauthorised access, loss or misuse.

5. Reviews and User-Generated Content

Users may submit reviews and ratings relating to places and attractions featured in KosovaGo.

Reviews may be displayed publicly within the application. Please avoid including sensitive personal information in content intended for public display.

We may moderate or remove reviews that violate applicable laws or our content guidelines.

6. Location and Search

KosovaGo allows users to explore destinations by selecting cities and browsing available listings.

The current version of the application does not require access to your device's precise location to provide its core discovery features.

Searches performed using the application's local search functionality are used to filter available listings.

7. Third-Party Services

KosovaGo uses third-party technology providers to operate certain features of the application.

These include:

  • Supabase: Account authentication, database services and application data storage.
  • OpenAI: Processing AI assistant requests and generating travel recommendations.
  • Apple: Distribution of the iOS application and associated platform services.
  • Hosting Providers: Hosting and delivery of our website and application services.

These providers may process information necessary to deliver their respective services, subject to their applicable privacy terms and contractual arrangements.

8. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the application, maintain accounts, comply with applicable legal obligations and address legitimate security or operational requirements.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the services involved.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable data protection laws, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated personal information.
  • Object to or request restrictions on certain processing activities.
  • Request information about how your personal data is handled.

To exercise your rights or request account deletion, please contact us using the email address below.

We may need to verify your identity before processing certain requests.

10. Children's Privacy

KosovaGo is a general travel and discovery application and is not specifically directed towards children.

If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us so that we can review the situation.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to our services, legal requirements or data processing practices.

Any updates will be published on this page with a revised date.

12. Contact Us

For privacy enquiries, account deletion requests or questions about how your information is handled, contact:
